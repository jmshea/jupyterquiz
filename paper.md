Title: "JupyterQuiz: An Interactive Quiz Generator for Jupyter Notebooks and Jupyter Book"

Author: John M. Shea

Affiliation: University of Florida, Gainesville, Florida, USA

Description: JupyterQuiz is a tool for displaying interactive self-assessment
quizzes in Jupyter notebooks and Jupyter Book. This tools is motivated by two
educational principles: formative assessment (cf. [@black2009developing])
and spaced repetition [@CITATION NEEDED].

In terms of formative assessment, this tool is designed to 1) allow a learner to
test themselves on material being learned outside of a summative (for instance, graded) 
activity, and 2) allow instructional designers to encode feedback into the question 
responses to provide guidance to a student who answers a question incorrectly.
